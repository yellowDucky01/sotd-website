Getting Started on SOTD

Welcome to the official State of the Dead Wiki. Here you will find all the custom mechanics and features that make our server unique.

First Steps: Unlike vanilla survival, our infected are slightly faster and hit harder, but drop unique loot such as bandages or low-tier ammo. Do not engage them without a melee weapon.

The Map

We use a custom modified version of Chernarus where nature has completely taken over. Major highways are blocked by abandoned convoys, and thick forests hide military bunkers requiring color-coded keycards to access.

Parties

Press P in-game to open the party menu. You can add up to 3 friends to your party to see their name tags from a short distance and prevent friendly fire with explosives.
