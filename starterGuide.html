STARTER SURVIVAL GUIDE

1

The Fresh Spawn

Upon waking up on the coast, your absolute first priority is finding clean water and warm clothes. Check small rowboats along the beach for emergency supplies.

2

Infected Zones

Stay alert. Major towns have high concentrations of infected that can overwhelm you quickly. Sneak past them or use suppressed weapons you find at military checkpoints.

3

Base Building

Gather a hatchet, handsaw, and nails (often found in industrial zones). Use our custom base building system (hit 'B' in-game) to secure a perimeter. Remember, bases can only be raided on weekends!

4

Safe Zone Traders

Located at Green Mountain. Leave your weapons on your back. Here you can sell pelts, rare loot, and buy vehicles or heavy weaponry from the community merchants.
