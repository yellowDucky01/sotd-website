DONATION PERKS EXPLAINED

We believe in keeping a level playing field. Our donation perks are strictly restricted to Pay-for-Convenience and Pay-to-Look-Cool. We do not sell guns, armor, or unraidable bases. Here is an explanation of what the perks entail.

Priority Queue

During wipe day, the server gets extremely full. Priority queue allows you to bypass the standard line and join much faster.

Custom Base Flag

Send an image block to admins on Discord, and we will upload it so you can proudly fly your faction's flag outside your base.

Supply Drop Flare

A once-a-day item. When thrown, it calls in a care package containing food, water, building materials, and clothing. No top-tier weapons are included to prevent P2W.
