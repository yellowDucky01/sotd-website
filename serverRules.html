SERVER RULES

Failure to adhere to these rules will result in warnings, kicks, or a permanent ban depending on severity.

1. No Cheating or Exploiting

Use of aimbots, ESP, duping glitches, or falling under the map to raid bases is strictly prohibited and results in an immediate permanent ban without appeal.

2. Team Limits (Max 4)

You may only roam, raid, base, or trade in groups of 4 or fewer. If an alliance occurs during a fight making it 5+ vs X, you will be struck. "We were just trading" is not an excuse outside safezones.

3. Safezone Rules

No camping outside the safezone radius (must be 400m away). No stealing from vehicles inside the safezone. No mic spamming in the trader area.

4. Toxicity & Racism

Trash talk is allowed and expected in PvP games. However, racism, sexism, real-life threats, or doxxing will lead to an instant and permanent ban from both Discord and Server.

5. Base Raiding

Raid times are Friday 6:00 PM EST to Sunday 11:59 PM EST. Offline raiding is allowed during this window. Boosting into bases is allowed as long as you do not use glitches/exploits.
