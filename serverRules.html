PvP/PvE Rules

  • PvP is ENABLED at all times, except in designated safe zones(Muldraugh Only)
  • No combat logging (logging out to avoid death or capture).
  • Shooting inside your own car is also allowed
  • No spawn killing or camping spawn/safe zones.
  • No exploiting game mechanics (animation abuse, glitches, dupes).

Factions & Territory

  • Factions must have at least 2 members to be considered active.
  • Offline raiding is NOT allowed unless approved by admins.

Raiding & Combat

  • Raiding is allowed only during raid hours (as announced by admins).
  • No griefing: destroying bases without looting is prohibited.
  • No excessive wall stacking or unreachable sky bases.
  • Use of explosives follows admin-set limits.

Safe Zones

  • Safe zones are strictly no PvP.
  • No baiting or luring zombies into safe zones.
  • Stealing or scouting for raids inside safe zones is forbidden.

Gameplay Fairness

  • No hacking, cheating, or third-party tools.
  • No alt accounts for scouting, storage, or combat advantage.

Community Conduct

  • No toxic behavior (harassment, racism, hate speech).
  • Trash talk is allowed—but keep it reasonable.

Admin Authority

  • Admin decisions are final.
  • Abuse of admin tools results in permanent ban.
  • Appeals must be submitted via ticket system.

Punishments

  • 1st offense: Warning
  • 2nd offense: Temporary ban
  • Severe offenses: Permanent ban

Safehouse Raiding Rules

  • Safehouse War Declaration Required
  • The objective of a Safehouse War is to fight for control, not to raid or take stored items.

Event Faction War Exception

  • Looting safehouses is ONLY allowed during official Event Faction Wars.
  • During these events, factions may loot other players’ safehouses only within the event’s specified time window.
