PvP/PvE Rules

  • PvP is ENABLED at all times, except in designated safe zones(Muldraugh Only)
  • No combat logging (logging out to avoid death or capture).
  • Shooting inside your own car is also allowed
  • No spawn killing or camping spawn/safe zones.
  • No exploiting game mechanics (animation abuse, glitches, dupes).

Factions & Territory

  • Factions must have at least 2 members to be considered active.
  • Offline raiding is NOT allowed unless approved by admins.

Raiding & Combat

  • Raiding is allowed only during raid hours (as announced by admins).
  • No griefing: destroying bases without looting is prohibited.
  • No excessive wall stacking or unreachable sky bases.
  • Use of explosives follows admin-set limits.

Safe Zones

  • Safe zones are strictly no PvP.
  • No baiting or luring zombies into safe zones.
  • Stealing or scouting for raids inside safe zones is forbidden.

Gameplay Fairness

  • No hacking, cheating, or third-party tools.
  • No alt accounts for scouting, storage, or combat advantage.

Community Conduct

  • No toxic behavior (harassment, racism, hate speech).
  • Trash talk is allowed—but keep it reasonable.

Admin Authority

  • Admin decisions are final.
  • Abuse of admin tools results in permanent ban.
  • Appeals must be submitted via ticket system.

Punishments

  • 1st offense: Warning
  • 2nd offense: Temporary ban
  • Severe offenses: Permanent ban

Safehouse Raiding Rules

  • Safehouse War Declaration Required
    • Before raiding a player's safehouse, you must first declare a Safehouse War to the player you want to raid. Raiding without declaring a war is not allowed.
  • No Griefing
    • Destroying or damaging bases is strictly prohibited. Raiding must have a purpose (Looting player Bodies around their Houses), not just destruction.
  • No Use of Explosives
    • Explosives are not allowed when raiding safehouses.
  • No Spawn Killing or Spawn Camping
    • Players must not kill or wait for players at spawn points or designated safe zones.
  • No Combat Logging
    • Logging out during combat to avoid death, capture, or consequences is not allowed.

No Intensional Base Camping

Why It’s Restricted?

  • Unfair Advantage
    • Camping right near an enemy spawn or base can make it impossible for them to play normally.
  • Griefing / Harassment
    • Constant attacks or blocking resources can ruin the game experience for others.
  • Game Balance
    • It can break intended game mechanics

Gameplay Fairness

  • No hacking, cheating, or third-party tools.
    • Any Player Caught Will be listed in the BAN LIST CHANNEL! (DISCORD CHANNEL)

Reporting Violations

If a player is caught breaking the rules and another player has clear video evidence showing how the incident happened, it can be submitted to the admins for review

  • The video must clearly show
  • The player committing the violation
  • The situation and how it happened
  • The player’s name visible if possible

Punishment

  • 1st Offense: Warning
  • 2nd Offense: Temporary Ban
  • Severe Offenses: Permanent Ban

Safehouse War Rule – Defender Faction Notice All players and factions acting as Defenders should be aware of the following rule regarding Safehouse Wars

  • If an Attacker faction/player claims your safehouse for a Safehouse War, the war will automatically activate once the claim is made.
  • This will apply even if the defending player or faction declines the war request.
  • Defenders should be prepared to defend their safehouse once it has been claimed.

Anti-Griefing Limitation

To prevent abuse or repetitive attacks

  • Each attacker player or faction is limited to a maximum of 3
  • Safehouse Wars against the same target safehouse/faction.
  • Repeated attacks beyond this limit may be considered griefing and will be subject to administrative action.

Safehouse War – Attacker Faction Rules

All factions or players initiating a Safehouse War (Attackers) must follow these rules during the war

  • Do not claim a safehouse that is close to another player’s safehouse.
  • Bombs or any explosive devices are strictly prohibited during Safehouse Wars.
  • Respawn killing / spawn camping is not allowed. Do not repeatedly kill players at their respawn point.
  • Do not build stairs, structures, or any objects that give an unfair advantage during the war.
    • Any violation of these rules may result in administrative action or penalties.

Trashtalk Rule

Trashtalk is only allowed in-game as part of competitive gameplay. Keep it within the game environment and do not take it personally.

  • Trashtalking in Discord is NOT allowed under any circumstances.
  • Respect Still Applies
    • Hate speech, harassment, excessive toxicity, or real-life threats are strictly prohibited anywhere, including in-game.
    Failure to follow this rule may result in warnings, mutes, or bans.

RAIDING / FACTION CLAIM WAR CLARIFICATION

To avoid confusion regarding Safehouse Claim Wars, please read the following

  • Faction Claim War (Safehouse War)
    • Once you claim a Safehouse War against another faction, you are NOT allowed to loot or steal supplies from that faction’s safehouse.
    • The objective of a Safehouse War is to fight for control, not to raid or take stored items.
  • Event Faction War Exception
    • Looting safehouses is ONLY allowed during official Event Faction Wars.
    • During these events, factions may loot other players’ safehouses only within the event’s specified time window.
  • Offline Protection During Events
    • If all members of a faction are offline, their safehouse cannot be raided or looted, even during an Event Faction War.

Muldraugh PVE Zone Regulations

  • Prohibited in the Muldraugh PVE Zone
    • No baiting or luring zombies into Muldraugh
    • No stealing, griefing, or causing chaos
    • No actions that intentionally disrupt the peace of the PVE community
    • No camping/scouting around the exit/outside borders of Muldraugh
  • Roleplay-Friendly Zone
    • Because Muldraugh is PVE, it functions similarly to an RP-safe zone.
    • Respect the environment and the players who choose to live and build here.
  • Outside Muldraugh
    • Beyond the borders of Muldraugh, the other cities are no longer under our jurisdiction.
    • Players are free to act as they wish outside the PVE zone.
